## Full description — both stores [4000]

```
Cities is the classic word game of naming cities, played against CityBot.

CityBot names a city. You answer with a city that starts with its last letter: Kyiv → Vilnius → Seoul → Lima… Each city can be used only once. Keep the chain going before your timer runs out, and win when CityBot runs out of cities it knows.

TWO CITY LISTS
• Ukraine: over 800 Ukrainian cities and towns.
• World: more than 30,000 cities from over 240 countries.

THREE DIFFICULTIES
• Easy: CityBot knows only the best-known cities, and you get 90 seconds a turn.
• Medium: a bigger vocabulary and 60 seconds.
• Hard: CityBot knows even little-known cities, and you get 40 seconds.

PLAYED FAIRLY
• Old and alternative names count: Kiev, Bombay, Saigon…
• Letters that hardly any city starts with are skipped, and the chat shows which letter comes next.
• Stuck? Use one of three hints, and CityBot plays a city for you.

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
• Score points for every city, with a bonus for cities you name for the first time.
• See your records for each list and difficulty, and how many cities you have discovered.

IN UKRAINIAN AND ENGLISH
Switch the language in Settings: the city names follow it.

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
No internet needed, no ads, no accounts, no data collected. Your progress stays on your device.

City data: GeoNames (geonames.org), licensed under CC BY 4.0.
```
